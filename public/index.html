Super Mario Galaxy,
together, online.

NebulaCoop adds online multiplayer to Nebula, the native PC port of Super Mario Galaxy, in the spirit of sm64coopdx. Several players share a galaxy, each with their own Mario or Luigi, over the internet, with no port forwarding.

Download for Windows How to join a room

Latest release · Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

You need your own copy of the game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.

Built on Nebula. Everything that makes the game run on a PC (the host, the renderers, Setup, controller and Wii Remote support, mods, achievements and the settings menu) is Nebula's, by keepvaibin. NebulaCoop follows it closely and adds co-op on top.

Playing together

Rooms of 2 to 16

One player hosts and the world runs on their PC. Everyone else joins with a room code.

No port forwarding

Rooms go through NebulaNet, NebulaCoop's own CoopNet-style matchmaker. Direct address:port still works too.

Mario or Luigi

Each player picks their character and can switch any time, with their own animations.

Per-mission instances

You play with whoever is on the same galaxy and star. The galaxy and star selects show who is where.

A shared world

Enemies, switches and boss fights are the room's. Coins and star bits are your own, but gone for everyone once taken.

Bubble on death

Instead of losing a life, float in a bubble and fly to another player to get back in.

Chat and commands

Chat, nametags, a player list, pop-ups, and /kick, /ban and /moderator for hosts.

The room's mods

The host picks the mods. Joiners download any they're missing from the host as they join.

Install

  1. Download Nebula-Setup.exe from the latest release. It isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen says "Unknown publisher": choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Select your copy of the game and choose Install. If Nebula is already installed, Setup can import its game and saves.
  3. Start NebulaCoop from the Start menu.

Run Setup again to update, repair or uninstall. Updates are signed, and Setup checks the signature before installing.

Requirements

  • Your own Super Mario Galaxy disc image (ISO, RVZ or extracted folder), revision 0: North America (RMGE01), Europe (RMGP01) or Japan (RMGJ01)
  • Windows 10 (1803 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit
  • A Direct3D 12 GPU and a CPU with AVX2
  • 8 GB of RAM
  • An internet connection to play online

Everyone in a room needs the same NebulaCoop version and a supported disc.

Join a room

1

Set up your player

Open Options › PLAYER and set your name and character.

2

Host

Choose Host, pick or create a save, and leave NETWORK on NebulaNet. Set LOBBY to Public to list the room for anyone, or keep it Friends only. The room code shows in chat and on the ONLINE tab.

3

Join

Choose Join and enter the room code, or pick a room under ROOMS: Public for everyone's public rooms, Private for rooms with the password you typed.

To leave, use the pause menu's Quit. Only the host's Quit saves the room's game. Prefer a VPN or a forwarded port? Pick NETWORK Direct and share address:port (UDP 7777 by default).

Co-op mods

NebulaCoop runs Nebula's .luma mods, plus co-op mods packed as .lumacoop. Co-op mods use LumaScript's coop.* calls, modeled on sm64coopdx's Lua API, to see who is in the room and react as players join, leave and move between galaxies.

LumaLink Coop converts mods and texture packs to .luma and .lumacoop files. The modding reference is in docs/modding.

Built-in mods

  • Personal Star Counter: each player's stars this room and all time, from sm64coopdx. The host turns it on in Mods.
  • Character Movesets: Luigi's own physics from Super Mario Galaxy, off by default so both characters play the same.
