YOUR GAME

Game files

NebulaCoop includes no game files. You need Super Mario Galaxy for Wii, revision 0: North America (RMGE01), Europe (RMGP01) or Japan (RMGJ01), as an ISO, RVZ or extracted folder. Everyone in a room needs their own copy.
