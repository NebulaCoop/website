NEBULACOOP

What is NebulaCoop?

NebulaCoop is a fork of Nebula, the native PC port of Super Mario Galaxy, that adds online multiplayer in the spirit of sm64coopdx. Several players share a galaxy, each with their own character, over the internet. You need your own copy of the game.

Online

Rooms and room codes →

Rooms of 2 to 16 players. Join by room code through NebulaNet, with no port forwarding, or connect directly.

Players

Characters and colours →

Mario, Luigi or a mod's character, with your own colours. Everyone in the room sees them.

Online

Per-mission instances →

You play with whoever is on the same galaxy and star. The galaxy and star selects show who is where.

World

A shared world →

Enemies, switches and boss fights are the room's. Coins and Star Bits are yours, but gone for everyone once taken.

Room options

Bubble on death →

Float in a bubble instead of losing a life, and fly to another player to get back in. Plus player interaction, PvP and cheats.

In the room

Chat and commands →

Chat, nametags, a player list, pop-ups, and /kick, /ban and /moderator for the host.

Modding

Co-op mods →

LumaScript's coop calls and events, .lumacoop files, mod characters and colours. The room shares its mods.

Nebula

Everything from Nebula →

Keyboard, controllers and the Wii Remote, Direct3D 12 and Vulkan, upscalers, mods, achievements and saves.

What co-op adds

NebulaCoop is built on Nebula by keepvaibin and follows it closely. Everything that runs the game on a PC is Nebula's.

START PLAYING

Getting started

  1. 01

    Check your specs

    Windows 10 (1803 or newer) or 11, 64-bit, a CPU with AVX2, a Direct3D 12 GPU and 8 GB RAM. Everyone in a room needs the same NebulaCoop version.

  2. 02

    Get your game

    You need Super Mario Galaxy for Wii, revision 0: North America (RMGE01), Europe (RMGP01) or Japan (RMGJ01), as an ISO, RVZ or extracted folder.

  3. 03

    Install, then host or join

    Install with NebulaCoop Setup, open NebulaCoop from the Start menu, set your name on the PLAYER page, then Host a room or Join one by its code.

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQs

Is NebulaCoop free?

Yes. NebulaCoop, like Nebula, is free software under the GNU GPL v3. Everyone who plays needs their own copy of Super Mario Galaxy.

How is it different from Nebula?

NebulaCoop is Nebula with online co-op added: rooms, shared worlds, chat, co-op mods and more. It follows Nebula closely and brings in its updates. Co-op problems belong on NebulaCoop's issue tracker, not Nebula's.

Do I need to forward ports?

No. Rooms go through NebulaNet, NebulaCoop's room-code service, by default. Direct connections to an address and port (UDP 7777) still work, with a forwarded port or a VPN such as Tailscale.

Which copy of the game do I need?

Super Mario Galaxy for Wii, revision 0: North America (RMGE01), Europe (RMGP01) or Japan (RMGJ01), as an ISO, RVZ or extracted folder. Everyone in a room needs a supported disc.

How many players can play?

2 to 16 per room, 8 by default. The host picks the number. The world runs on the host's computer.

Can I keep Nebula installed?

Yes. NebulaCoop installs beside Nebula with its own folders and saves. The first install offers to import Nebula's game and saves.

Which platforms does it run on?

Windows. Nebula's Linux, macOS and Android versions are not ready in NebulaCoop yet.

Is NebulaCoop made by Nintendo?

No. NebulaCoop is an independent project, not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo.
