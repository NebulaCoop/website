PRIVACY

Privacy

NebulaCoop checks its GitHub releases for updates when it starts; Check at start on the SYSTEM page turns this off. DLSS and XeSS download from NVIDIA and Intel only when you choose them. Discord status connects only to the Discord app on your computer.

Mods ask permission before using the internet, your files, the camera or the microphone.

Online play: the players in a room see your name, character and colours, and connect to your computer’s address. With NebulaNet, your game asks Cloudflare’s STUN server for its public address, and the NebulaNet server keeps a room’s name, code, settings and the host’s addresses while it is open, so players can find it, plus hourly counts of rooms and players. It has no accounts. Bans and moderators are kept by IP address on the host’s computer.

Crash reports and diagnostics recordings stay on your computer unless you attach them to a report. This website has no analytics or accounts. Your browser reads the release and download counts from GitHub. External sites such as GitHub and Discord have their own privacy policies.
