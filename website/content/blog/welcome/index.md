NebulaCoop now has its own website. It is built from [Nebula's website](https://nebula.keepvaibin.com/) by keepvaibin, with a look of its own and everything co-op added on top.

Here is what you can find on it:

- [What co-op adds](/coop/): rooms and room codes, characters and colours, the shared world and the room options.
- [Playing online](/play/online/): setting up your player, hosting, joining, chat commands and what to try when you can't connect.
- [The modding docs](/modding/docs/), with a new [co-op section](/modding/docs/coop/) for LumaScript's `coop` calls, `.lumacoop` files, mod characters and colours.
- [Downloads](/download/), always pointing at the newest release.

NebulaCoop is built on Nebula and follows it closely, so most of what this site says about graphics, controls, mods and saves comes straight from Nebula. If something here is wrong for NebulaCoop, [open an issue](https://github.com/NebulaCoop/nebula-coop/issues) or tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/hNbgFtaRHv).
